# Solo OS Team

Meet your Solo OS team and hire each employee into your workspace, starting with your Manager.

## Getting started

1. Open **Team** in the dock.
2. Copy the access link from an employee's lesson in the course, paste it into **Access link**, and choose **Unlock**.
3. Choose **Employ me** on their card. They join your agents in the selected community, ready to chat.
4. Add the next employee's link when you reach their lesson. Solo OS remembers the links you have pasted.
